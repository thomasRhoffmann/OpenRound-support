# OpenRound Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 2026

**OpenRound** (“OpenRound”) is developed by Thomas Hoffmann. This policy describes how the app handles information.

## Summary

OpenRound is designed to keep your scoring data **on your devices**. We do not operate servers that store your rounds, scores, or player names. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. There is no account and no sign-in.

## Information you enter in the app

You may enter information such as:

- Player and guest names
- Scores, round dates, and course selections
- Per-hole par and distance overrides
- Custom sketched courses and layouts
- Unit preference (feet or meters)
- Optional iCloud sync preference (iPhone)

This information is stored **locally on your device** using Apple’s SwiftData framework (and UserDefaults for lightweight settings). It is not transmitted to us.

## Location

OpenRound may request **location while you use the app** to sort nearby courses when you are choosing where to play. Location is processed **on device only** for that sorting. Your location is **not stored** by OpenRound and is **not transmitted** to us.

## iCloud sync (optional, iPhone only)

iCloud sync is **off by default**. If you turn it on in iPhone Settings, your rounds sync through **your** Apple iCloud account to your other devices signed into the same account, using Apple’s CloudKit. That sync is between your devices and Apple’s iCloud service — **not** to OpenRound or Thomas Hoffmann.

The Apple Watch app does not offer an iCloud toggle and keeps scoring data local on the Watch for the round you are playing.

## Apple Watch

The Watch app is intended for **live scoring during a round**. It does not maintain long-term round history. Round history and stats live on iPhone when you use both apps together. During an active round, iPhone and Watch may exchange scoring data over **WatchConnectivity** (a direct link between your paired devices).

## Bundled course directory

OpenRound includes a bundled course directory sourced from **[DiscGolfAPI](https://discgolfapi.com)** (course names, locations, and related metadata). That data ships inside the app so the directory works offline. DiscGolfAPI is a third-party data provider; OpenRound displays their directory data with attribution in the app. We do not send your personal scoring data to DiscGolfAPI.

## Information we do not collect

OpenRound does **not**:

- Require an account or login
- Upload your rounds or scores to our servers
- Use analytics or advertising SDKs
- Track you across other apps or websites
- Collect location on a server or store location history

## When data leaves your device

Data only leaves your device when **you choose** to, or when **Apple’s services** you enable handle it:

- **Share sheet** — scorecard images, CSV exports, or backup files you share via Mail, Messages, AirDrop, or other apps (you control recipients and content)
- **iCloud sync** — if you opt in on iPhone, Apple syncs your rounds via your iCloud account
- **WatchConnectivity** — during an active round, scores may sync between your paired iPhone and Apple Watch
- **Device backup** — iPhone or Apple Watch backup via iCloud or a computer (handled by Apple, not OpenRound)

We do not receive copies of shared or synced content.

## Notifications

OpenRound does **not** send push notifications from a server. The app does not register for remote push notifications.

## Children

OpenRound is a general-purpose sports scorecard. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children through our systems because we do not collect data on our servers.

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
